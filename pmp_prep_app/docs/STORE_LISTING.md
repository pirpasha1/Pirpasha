OpenPM Prep is a completely free study app for the PMP® (Project Management Professional) exam. No subscriptions, no ads, no locked content.

Built for the PMP exam launched in July 2026:
• Covers all three domains: People (33%), Process (41%), Business Environment (26%)
• A lesson for each of the 26 tasks in the official Exam Content Outline, with key terms, worked examples, predictive-vs-agile notes and exam tips
• Flashcards with spaced repetition, a formula sheet and a searchable glossary

Practice and mock exams:
• 10, 30 and 60-question practice tests, with instant feedback or results at the end
• Full mock exam in the current format: case studies first, two optional 10-minute breaks and section locking, like the real exam
• Question types: multiple choice, multiple response, matching, ordering and categorizing, plus case studies
• Question palette, mark for review, timer with automatic submission
• Detailed explanations: why the right answer is right and why each wrong answer is wrong

Track your progress:
• Readiness score, study time and lesson completion
• Results by domain, approach (predictive / agile / hybrid) and task
• Weak areas linked straight to the right lesson

Free resources:
• A library of verified free resources, including the official Exam Content Outline, the Scrum Guide and an open-licensed project management textbook
• A directory of project management institutions

Private by design: works fully offline, needs no account and collects no data.

All lessons and questions are original practice content written against the public Exam Content Outline. They are not real exam questions.

OpenPM Prep is an independent study app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Project Management Institute (PMI). PMP, PMBOK and PMI are registered marks of Project Management Institute, Inc.
