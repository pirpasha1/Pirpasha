# OpenPM Prep – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Applies to app version 0.1.x*

> Draft prepared for publication. Have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer for your jurisdiction before public launch.

OpenPM Prep ("the app") is a free, independent study app for the PMP® certification exam. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Project Management Institute (PMI). PMP, PMBOK and PMI are registered marks of Project Management Institute, Inc.

## What data the app collects
**None.** The current version of the app:
- has no user accounts and no sign-in;
- does not request internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device;
- contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software;
- does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or any other personal data.

## What is stored on your device
The app stores these on your device only, in its private storage:
- your study progress, test results and flashcard history;
- your settings (display name if you enter one, exam date, theme, text size);
- any partnership enquiry or question feedback you write.

This data never leaves your device through the app. Android's own device backup may include it if you have enabled backup in your Google account settings.

## Links to other websites
The Resources section links to third-party websites (for example pmi.org). They open in your browser and are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

## Deleting your data
Use **Profile → Delete all my data**, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the app stored.

## Children
The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional certification. It is not directed at children.

## Future versions
If a future version adds optional accounts, cloud sync or an AI assistant, this policy will be updated before release. It will describe exactly what is collected and why, and those features will be optional.
